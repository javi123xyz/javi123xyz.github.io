Commissioned Work

As a freelance graphic designer, I have been trusted by several of my friends and family to create ] graphic designs for them. Whether it's to help them brand their business, for their own creative endeavors, or simply for fun, I enjoy working with folks to bring their ideas to life. These are some of the designs I have worked on the past few years. If you are interested in working with me, please email me.

During my time working for The Roots Dispensary in Lompoc, CA, I played a key role in shaping the company’s visual identity. From 2019 to 2022, I worked on a wide range of graphic designs, including social media graphics, promotional flyers, and merchandise. My goal with these designs was to merge cannabis culture with clean, streetwear-inspired designs, helping elevate The Roots brand image while keeping it deeply rooted in its local community.
