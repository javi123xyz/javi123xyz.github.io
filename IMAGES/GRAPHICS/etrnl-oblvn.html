Eternal Oblivion

How do I begin to describe what Eternal Oblivion is? I first came up with the idea for an apparel and design brand some time in 2015 during my freshman year of college. The name derives from the philosophical concept of the "Eternal Oblivion". I have used this as a motif in my designs, blending it with other inspiration sources to come up with unique and attention grabbing designs. To me, Eternal Oblivion is more tahn just a design brand, it is also a reflection on existence, impermanence, and the art we leave behind. All of the designs below are available as prints.
